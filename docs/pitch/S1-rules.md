# S1: Organisers' rules, eligibility and deadline

Checked Tue 13 Oct 2026 by the Abdullah lane (fiona-fa). Status: **done, but the deadline finding changes the plan**.

## Summary

The official rules set **one deadline for registration and submission: Tuesday 20 October 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (UTC+8)**. That is **20:59 Pakistan time on 20 October** and 15:59 UTC. Our plan assumed Monday 2 November; the real date is 13 days earlier, and no changes are accepted after it. Submissions can be updated any number of times before the deadline, and the latest version counts. The review runs from 20 to 31 October, finalists are announced on 31 October, check-in is on 20 November, and the final and awards are on 21 to 23 November in Shenzhen. At least one member of a finalist team must attend in person.

**Eligibility is not a threat.** The rules have no APEC requirement. Every member must be a full-time student (undergraduate, master's or doctoral) at an accredited university or research institution outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR and Chinese Taipei. At least 60% of the team must be non-Chinese nationals, a team has 2 to 5 members, and each person may join only one team. Four Pakistani students at Pakistani universities qualify, **provided all four are currently enrolled full time**. Everyone should confirm this.

The official language is English for submissions, presentations and reviews. Our English interface is compliant; Urdu and Chinese input stays a feature, not a requirement.

## What we must submit (official checklist)

| # | Item | Requirement as written |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Technical documentation | System architecture, core algorithm description, security design |
| 02 | Presentation deck | Final presentation no longer than 10 minutes |
| 03 | Demo video | Optional, no longer than 5 minutes |
| 04 | Source code | With README, GitHub repository link, deployment instructions and test cases |
| 05 | Security self-assessment report | Permission-tier implementation and known-risk list |
| 06 | Execution evidence | For non-on-chain topics: simulated-account or sandbox operation logs and a demo video |

## How entries are scored

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Task completion | 40% |
| Security and compliance | 30% |
| Innovation and interaction | 30% |
| Challenge bonus (on-chain exploration, Topic F only in practice) | 0 to 5 points |

All six topics share the same criteria. Our topic is **Topic E, SME Finance Copilot**: "A finance copilot for small-business owners, offering financing-option matching, cash-flow management with alerts, and business financial analysis." Our debt-versus-equity decision, 13-week cash forecast with buffer alerts, and financial analysis map directly onto those three phrases.

## Registration

Download the registration form from the official website (fintechathon.g-ican.com), complete it, and email it to the official address. The organiser's contact in the rules document is wangyr@pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn; the IEEE page hides the registration address, so confirm it on the website. Use the subject line "International Track Registration – Country – Team Name – Team Leader's Full Name". The organiser promises a confirmation within 48 hours. Because registration closes at the same moment as submission, **register now**, so a problem with eligibility or paperwork surfaces while there is time to fix it. Only a team member can send this.

## What this changes for us

1. **The schedule.** Every gate after G1 (G2 on 18 Oct, G3 on 25 Oct, G4 on 29 Oct, G5 on 1 Nov) assumed 2 November. The orchestrator needs to re-plan around an internal freeze on Sunday 18 or Monday 19 October and submission on 20 October, with the slack before 20:59 PKT.
2. **Security counts for 30%.** Two of the six required items are about safety: the security self-assessment (permission tiers and known risks) and execution evidence from a sandbox. The Guardian, the mandates, the autonomy ladder and the hash-chained audit log are therefore scored directly, not just demonstrated.
3. **The video is optional and capped at 5 minutes; the deck is capped at 10.** B7 should produce both, but the deck and documentation are mandatory, and the video can reuse the demo run that also serves as execution evidence.
4. **Integrity and IP.** Third-party data, code and open-source components must be properly licensed. Public datasets (UCI Online Retail II, Favorita, M5, SEC EDGAR, filings.xbrl.org, Damodaran) need their licences recorded before we use them in the submission.
5. **Prizes.** International Track pool RMB 250,000: first prize RMB 100,000, second RMB 80,000, third RMB 50,000, Best Innovation Award RMB 20,000, plus several Excellence Awards. Prizes are pre-tax. Outstanding teams also get a fast-track interview with WeBank. Finalists' travel and accommodation are paid by the organiser.
